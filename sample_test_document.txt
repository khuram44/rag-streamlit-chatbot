BrightLeaf University — Student Handbook (Fictional)

1. About BrightLeaf University
BrightLeaf University is a fictional institution founded in 1994 in the
city of Rivermont. It currently enrolls approximately 8,200 students
across four schools: Business, Engineering, Arts & Sciences, and Health
Sciences. The university's mission is to provide affordable, high-quality
education with a strong focus on practical, career-ready skills.

2. Library Hours
The Main Library is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM,
and on weekends from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. During final exam week, the
library extends its hours and stays open 24 hours a day from Sunday
night through the following Friday.

3. Tuition and Fees
Undergraduate tuition for the 2026-2027 academic year is $9,400 per
semester for in-state students and $16,750 per semester for out-of-state
students. A one-time technology fee of $250 is charged to all new
students during their first semester. Graduate tuition rates are listed
separately in the Graduate Catalog.

4. Attendance Policy
Students are expected to attend at least 80% of scheduled class sessions
for each course. Instructors may deduct up to 5 percentage points from a
student's final grade for each unexcused absence beyond this threshold.
Excused absences require documentation submitted to the Registrar's
Office within five business days of the missed class.

5. Wi-Fi and Campus Network
Campus Wi-Fi is available in all academic buildings, residence halls,
and the student union. Students connect using the network name
"BrightLeaf-Secure" and their student ID credentials. The IT Help Desk
is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, for connectivity
issues.

6. Scholarship Program
The BrightLeaf Merit Scholarship awards up to $4,000 per year to
incoming freshmen with a high school GPA of 3.7 or above. Renewal
requires maintaining a cumulative university GPA of at least 3.3 and
completing a minimum of 24 credit hours per academic year.
